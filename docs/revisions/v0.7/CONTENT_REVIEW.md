# Source audit and editorial choices

The five supplied images are preserved unmodified in `references/`. They are content references, not UI backgrounds or a new art direction. The first image includes phone controls; those are never copied into the application.

## Consolidation

The source repeats several stations, numbers two different stations as 3, and places the seven-day ending before trust/patience. The implementation consolidates these into fifteen ordered stations and places the ending last. Faith/good-deeds material is retained as its own chapter inside Ma'iyya.

The school story keeps a fictional resolution but explicitly avoids promising that a child who believes will quickly see punishment or apologies. It includes seeking trusted help and fair listening. Children are not labelled inherently bad or good by their behavior in one incident.

The 24/7/38 branch counts are not used as quiz facts: they need an identified classification source. [Sahih Muslim 35b](https://sunnah.com/muslim:35b) supplies the broader branches-of-faith teaching and examples. The faith lesson includes the six foundations found in [the hadith of Jibril, Muslim 8a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:8a).

## Activity boundaries

- No measurement of sincerity, inner faith, taqwa, emotional calm or divine favor.
- The optional timer does not certify worship or require completion before learning continues.
- Daily prayer is a private self-report by ability/circumstances; the app neither verifies nor judges it.
- The dice is a virtual participation keepsake and does not depend on prayer reports. There are no actual gifts, purchases or stakes.
- Optional triangles select ordinary educational suggestions, not personal revelation or divine predictions.
- Written reflections stay optional and local. No voice recording, notification permission or backend is added.
