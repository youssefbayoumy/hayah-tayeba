# v0.7 — باب مَعِيَّة الله

26 October 2026. Third main journey, alongside the unchanged Dhikr opening sample and the three-garden journey. Ages 10–14, Arabic, no account, written private reflections and browser-local data.

## Confirmed choices

- Include **الإيمان والعمل الصالح** as a chapter inside Ma'iyya, not a fourth main journey.
- Keep the existing garden theme and two shared characters. The five new images are content references only; their blue/gold design and different cartoon characters do not replace the app identity.
- Sequential stations with no daily waiting. Surprise triangles are optional suggestions separate from learning unlocks.
- The dice gives an optional virtual participation keepsake, with no prayer score, physical-gift promise or lost progress.

## Implemented route

Fifteen stations in three chapters: discover the meaning (1), faith and good deeds (7), practical meanings of Ma'iyya (7). The seven-day plan closes the route, after trust and patience; duplicated station numbers in the source are consolidated.

Six verse-matching exercises retain references and context. Four are bound to stations; two more are optional in the library. Matching a suitable meaning and application answer opens an educational gate, with unlimited retries. Narrative choices allow exploring supportive feedback and continuing. Neither mechanism awards or measures Allah's special companionship.

An optional two-minute dhikr timer pauses/resumes and does not gate learning. Prayer planning is written, with no background alarm or new permission. Six-item **رحلتي اليوم** records the child's editable reports by local date. It becomes the returning landing screen after the child starts Ma'iyya. Notes stay optional; missed days never erase records, stations or keepsakes. The optional seven-day plan does not impose seven elapsed days on digital completion.

Schema 4 adds a blank third journey to schema 3 while preserving both existing journeys, writings, profile, media progress and the v0.5 archive. Earlier schemas still migrate. A `.backup-v3` copy joins earlier migration backups and is purged on deliberate writing deletion or reset.
