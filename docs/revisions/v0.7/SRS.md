## Ordered curriculum

1. Discover the meaning of Ma'iyya.
2. Growing faith: Quran, dhikr, knowing Allah's names, good deeds, companionship and reflection on creation.
3. Branches of faith: examples for heart, tongue and body, without unsupported per-category counts.
4. Intention before action, without scrutinizing or judging inner sincerity.
5. Choosing a good deed without a numerical virtue competition.
6. Choosing rightly when alone.
7. Kindness and help at home.
8. Taqwa and ihsan through a contextual verse-matching activity.
9. Dhikr with attention and an optional pause.
10. Planning prayer.
11. Facing fear with prayer, support and wise action.
12. Learning Al-'Alim, As-Sami' and Al-Basir in age-appropriate wording.
13. Taking practical steps and trusting Allah before an exam.
14. Patience, support and trying another step.
15. A seven-day invitation and **رفيق الطاعة والإحسان** learning keepsake.

Fasting and additional voluntary worship remain later content, adapted to age and ability. No new video was provided, so the new journey has no placeholder film requirement.
